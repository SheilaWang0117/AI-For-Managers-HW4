# P10 — A campus field journal

The storefront now uses a field-journal theme: cream paper texture, Yale navy, pale blue, and small gold accents.

| What changed | Why it helps shoppers |
| --- | --- |
| Larger Caslon headlines, readable DM Sans details, gold underlines, and stronger navy buttons | Separates the brand story from product facts and makes the next shopping action obvious. Existing fonts are reused. |
| A gridded hero with a tilted product “print,” catalogue label, and bulldog seal | Gives the store a recognizable campus identity while keeping a real product and its price prominent. |
| Three campus-moment cards: morning layers, library days, and game days | Connects clothes to familiar occasions; each card opens a working, filtered collection. |
| Framed product cards, price badges, recorded color labels, and a larger detail-photo treatment | Makes products easier to compare and inspect before choosing a size. Mobile featured cards swipe horizontally instead of hiding the third item. |
| A gold-trimmed Handsome Dan header, cream reply bubbles, and assistant name labels | Makes help feel part of the storefront and keeps the conversation visually organized. |
| Short entrance animations and restrained hover movement | Signals interactive items without continuous motion. Keyboard focus stays visible; reduced-motion settings disable animation. |

These choices aim to encourage browsing and confident product selection; sales impact has not been measured.

**Verified:** production build, 12 browser checks, four screen widths, collection links, keyboard scrolling, and reduced motion.
